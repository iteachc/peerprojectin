The Peer Project // Talk to Someone

A mentor can help you study. They cannot help with everything. If it has stopped being only about the syllabus, these lines are free and confidential, and the people who pick up are trained for this.

if you are in danger right now

Call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.

If you're perhaps thinking about suicide or self-harm, call Tele-MANAS on 14416 or 1-800-891-4416, any time, in your own language. AASRA is also open all night: 022 2754 6669.

More lines are below. The first three are open all day and all night.

helplines

Tele-MANAS

Run by the Government of India. Call 14416 or 1-800-891-4416, any time, in your own language. Anonymous, and a trained counsellor picks up first.

Vandrevala Foundation

Call or WhatsApp +91 9999 666 555, 24 hours a day.

AASRA

Call 022 2754 6669, 24 hours a day, in English or Hindi. Confidential.

iCall (TISS)

Call 9152987821, Monday to Saturday, 8 am to 9 pm. Run by the Tata Institute of Social Sciences, and it also does counselling by email.

Manodarpan

Run by the Ministry of Education, for students. Call 8448440632. Sources disagree on its hours, so if nobody picks up, try Tele-MANAS above.

if someone is hurting or harassing you, or you feel unsafe

At home, at school, on the way there, or online. It does not matter who it is, and it is not your fault.

Under 18, any gender

Childline

Call 1098, 24 hours a day, if you are being abused, bullied or harassed, at home or at school. It is being folded into 112, so if 1098 does not connect, call 112.

POCSO e-Box (NCPCR)

An online complaint form run by the child rights commission, for anyone under 18 who has been sexually abused or harassed, including online. Your identity is kept confidential. It is on the NCPCR homepage.

Women, any age

Women Helpline

Call 181, 24 hours a day. For domestic violence, harassment, or any time you feel unsafe.

National Commission for Women

Call 7827170170, 24 hours a day. They log your complaint and connect you to the police, health services or legal aid.

Men, any age

We could not find a government helpline dedicated to men, and most violence helplines in India are for women. Call 112 if you are in danger, and use Tele-MANAS or Vandrevala above to talk it through. Under 18, Childline and the POCSO e-Box above are for you.

if photos, videos or messages of you are being shared or used to threaten you

This happens to a lot of students, and being threatened with your own images is the other person's offence, not yours. Do not pay and do not delete the messages. Report it.

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

Choose the women or child option for sexual content or sextortion, and you can report anonymously. For urgent help, call 1930.

Take It Down

A free tool from a US child-safety charity (NCMEC) that helps get nude or sexual images or videos taken of you when you were under 18 removed, even if you are an adult now. You do not have to send the image to anyone. It does not work on encrypted apps.

StopNCII.org

The same idea for images where you were 18 or older. It blocks matching images on partner platforms such as Instagram and Facebook, and the image never leaves your device.

other kinds of trouble

Anti-Ragging Helpline

Call 1800-180-5522, 24 hours a day, if you are being ragged or harassed in college.

National Task Force student support list

The task force appointed by the Supreme Court on student mental health keeps a longer list of helplines, including legal aid and disability support.

NGOs worth knowing

Sneha Foundation

A suicide prevention organisation in Chennai, run by volunteers. Call +91 44 2464 0050 or +91 44 2464 0060, 24 hours a day. You can also walk in, 10 am to 8 pm every day, with no appointment.

Jagori

A women's resource centre in Delhi. Helpline: +91 11 2669 2700 (ext 34 or 35) or +91 8800 996640, Monday to Friday, 9:30 am to 5:30 pm. Counselling and legal referrals for women facing violence.

CyberPeace Foundation

A cyber safety non-profit with a helpline for victims. Call +91 95700 00066 or email helpline@cyberpeace.net. File a police complaint on the cyber crime portal above as well.

The Live Love Laugh Foundation helpline list

A longer list of mental health helplines by city and language. They say they do not run these lines and cannot guarantee them, so check a number before you rely on it. The ones above are the ones we checked.

if it is not an emergency, just heavy

Talk to a school counsellor, a doctor, or someone at home. Finding exam prep too much is common, and it does not mean you are behind.

TPP mentors are students, not counsellors. We can help you with studying. For anything serious, the lines above are the right people.

That said, if you just want to vent, you can message @peerprojectin on Discord. We can't promise a quick reply, so if it can't wait, use the lines above.

Numbers last checked in October 2026. If one of them has changed or stopped working, tell us on Discord (@peerprojectin) or open a PR.
